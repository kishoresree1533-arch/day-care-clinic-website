# Day Care Clinic Website

Build a stunning, award-winning, multi-page website for DAY CARE CLINIC, located at Sathy Road, Kovilpalayam, Coimbatore – 641107.

Design: Premium international healthcare branding, elegant white + soft teal + deep navy palette, sophisticated typography, spacious layouts, stunning realistic medical photography, custom illustrations and refined micro-interactions. Avoid generic AI website templates.

Pages: Home, About Us, Our Services, Treatments, Facilities, Doctors & Team, Gallery and Contact Us.

Homepage: Cinematic hero with premium healthcare imagery, compelling headline, 24/7 availability badge, Book Appointment and Call Now buttons. Add trust indicators, clinic introduction, services, treatment highlights, facilities, patient-focused care, testimonials and location section.

Experience: Smooth Lenis scrolling, GSAP ScrollTrigger animations, Framer Motion transitions, elegant parallax, staggered reveals, interactive cards and premium hover effects. Create a unique editorial-style layout with exceptional visual storytelling.

Functionality: Fully responsive, mobile-first, working navigation, appointment enquiry form, click-to-call, WhatsApp integration, Google Maps and SEO optimization.

Content: Write professional, trustworthy, patient-friendly healthcare copy. Highlight 24/7 availability and accessible general healthcare. Never invent doctor names, qualifications, specialties or medical claims.

Use the uploaded clinic signage as branding reference. Maintain consistent design across all pages. Make the final result feel like a premium healthcare brand designed by a top international digital agency.
